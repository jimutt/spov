## Context

A session is named `fly-…` or `job-<id>`, which says nothing of what it is about. OpenCode generates a title for a conversation once a person has written in it and announces it with `session.renamed`; the recorder already keeps it in `conversations.title`. Measured on stored sessions: of 14 job sessions, the 2 a person wrote in have a title and the 12 nobody wrote in have none, so OpenCode alone titles interactive sessions and steered jobs but not an unattended job.

## Decision

- A session's title is the title of its main conversation (`store.Primary`, the oldest root) when that is not empty; otherwise, for a job's session, the job's own title (DL-150); otherwise none. A subagent's conversation never supplies it, and a session with no root conversation takes its job's title or none.
- `SessionView` and `JobView` carry it as `title`, omitted when there is none; a job's is its session's. The listing, the read of one session and a resume's answer carry it alike. It is read from the store for a whole listing page in one query (`ConversationStore.Titles`, keyed by session, beside `LastMessages`, scoped by org), never from a sandbox, and the session-state stream's stamp includes it so a list row updates when a title arrives. A failed read is warned about and leaves each title to its job, as a failed `LastMessages` leaves the preview out.
- The recorder writes a conversation's title from the sandbox's listing, from the one info read of a conversation first seen mid-log, and from each `session.renamed` frame when it arrives, so a rename during a live capture is in the store at once rather than at the next attach.
- A title is text a model wrote. It is stored and served as one line of at most 120 characters: control characters and line breaks become spaces, the format characters (Unicode category Cf: the bidirectional controls, zero-width spaces, word joiners, soft hyphens, tags) are removed except a zero-width joiner or non-joiner between two visible characters, runs of whitespace collapse, and the rest is cut at the bound (`store.CleanTitle`, applied where a title is written, for OpenCode's and a job's alike, and again where a view is built, so a row stored before this rule is served the same way). A title with nothing visible left is no title. No CLI table or message prints it; only `--json` carries it, as any other field, and a print added later goes through `printableLine` (DL-136). The browser draws it as text.
- Nothing addresses a session by its title: the name and the id stay the references in every route, command and log line.
- The CLI's tables do not show it.

## Rationale

OpenCode's title is written from the conversation as it went, so it is the better one whenever it exists, and it costs nothing. A job's own title fills the case OpenCode leaves empty. One bounded, single-line form at the write keeps every reader simple.

## Consequences

- A resumed or imported conversation keeps its title, since the import already carries it.
- A session's title can change during its life (OpenCode renames, a person renames from the attached client); the list follows.
