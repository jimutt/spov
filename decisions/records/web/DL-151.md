## Context

DL-106 and DL-147 made the repository the headline of a session's list row and of the session page's bar, because a session's name says little. A session can now have a title (DL-149).

## Decision

- `sessionHeadline` (beside `repoHeadline` in `format.ts`) decides what leads: the title when the session has one, else the repository, else the name.
- In a list row with a title, the headline is the title on one truncated line, with the whole title in its `title` attribute, and the meta line is the kind, the repository (`owner/name`, "+N" for more) and the session's name. Without a title the row is DL-147's, unchanged.
- The session page's bar follows the same rule: the title leads when there is one, and on the line below it the repository (`repoLine`) takes the name's place, the name being in the details sheet. That line holds the state, the reason or the running time, and the repository, which truncates and gives way before the reason does; it never overflows. The heading from `lg` is the title when there is one, and the name then joins its facts line; without a title the heading is the name, as before.
- The jobs page's rows show the job's title in place of the start of its goal when there is one (`jobHeadline`); the goal stays on the session page. A row's headline is clamped to two lines at every width.
- The page's document title (`<title>`) leads with the session's title when there is one ("<title> — spov", `documentTitle`), and is "Session — spov" otherwise.
- A title that arrives while the session page is open shows at once: a `session.renamed` frame of a root conversation in the log re-reads the session, once per batch of frames (`renamesMain`), a store read. The list follows through its stream.
- Nothing lets a reader edit a title.

## Rationale

A title says what a session is about, which neither the repository nor the name does; the repository still tells rows apart when several share a title or have none, so it stays on the row.

## Consequences

- Rows of one list can lead with different things (a title, a repository, a name). The meta line always carries the kind first, so rows still scan in a column.
- A title arriving while the list is open changes a row's headline in place.
