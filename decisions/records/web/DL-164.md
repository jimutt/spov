## Context

The sessions and jobs pages show each concurrency limit as a count and a row of up to 24 ticks (DL-060). Two things about that row did not hold up.

The fill was one tick per unit in use. That is exact while the limit is 24 or fewer. Above the cap the row has fewer ticks than units, so the 24th used unit filled the last tick: a limit of 500 showed a full row at 24 in use, while the count beside it said `24 of 500`.

The width was not bounded with the count. A full tick is 10 px with a 2 px gap, so 24 of them are 286 px before the label. Measured in Firefox at a 390 px viewport, the label wrapped and the row stood 56.6 px tall; at 320 px the summary line overflowed the 288 px content width by 61 px. Two meters on the sessions page already take a line each on a phone, so the row has to fit one meter and its words.

## Decision

The number of ticks is still `min(limit, 24)`. Two rules keep it usable:

- **Fill.** The number filled is `round(min(used, limit) / limit * ticks)`, so each tick is exactly one unit at or below the cap and a share of the limit above it.
- **Size.** A row of more than 16 ticks uses 6 px ticks with a 1 px gap below `sm`, where 16 full ticks fit the narrowest phone. A row of 16 or fewer keeps the 10 px tick and 2 px gap at every width.

A zero limit still draws no ticks, and the text remains the exact `used of limit`. `limitmeter.svelte.test.ts` covers a limit under the cap, one at it, a large one and no limit, checking the count, the filled ticks and the phone-size classes.

## Rationale

The cap is what keeps the summary line one bounded row (DL-060); the fill has to follow the cap or the row lies about how much of a large limit is in use, and the tick size has to follow it or the row cannot fit beside its own count on a phone. Proportional filling needs no new markup, keeps the exact per-unit reading for every limit the defaults use, and leaves the count as the authoritative figure. Shrinking only rows over 16 leaves the small limits a phone is likely to show — including the 5-and-4 defaults — at the original tick size.

## Consequences

- With a limit above 24, a small usage can light no tick because it is less than half a tick's share; the count still names it.
- Below `sm`, a large limit's ticks are 6 px by 4 px with a 1 px gap. They read as a fine dashed row rather than 24 separate squares, which is what lets it sit on one line at 390 px and wrap the label without overflowing at 320 px.
- A limit at or below 16 is drawn exactly as before, so the current defaults look unchanged at every width.
