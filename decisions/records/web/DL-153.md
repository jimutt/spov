## Context

DL-056's light theme was a stone ground: a mid-light paper (`#ebeae6`) with a lighter raised surface for the header and controls, and rows and chips that lightened to the raised surface on hover. The ground read as heavy.

## Decision

- The light theme's surfaces are: paper `#f9f9f7`, raised `#ffffff`, sunk `#f0f0ec`, rule `#e3e2de`, rule-2 `#cbc9c2`. The paper keeps a slight warm tint so the olive, rust and gold accents still sit on a related ground.
- The order of the surfaces is unchanged: raised is lighter than the paper and sunk is darker.
- A hover on the paper darkens to the sunk surface (`hover:bg-sunk`): list rows, the jobs filter chips and an unselected result card. The raised surface is for what stands on the page (the header, inputs, cards), not for a hover, since white on this paper is too small a step to see.
- Text and accent colours, the dark theme and DL-056's contrast rule are unchanged; `theme.test.ts` still checks every text colour on the paper and the raised surface.

## Rationale

A brighter ground reads as fresher and raises every text colour's contrast. With the paper two points from white, a lighter hover is invisible, and darkening is the only direction left with room.

## Consequences

- Inline code and wells on the sunk surface are a smaller step from the page than before.
- A new hover on the paper uses the sunk surface.
