## Context

DL-106 made a session's repository the headline of its row in the session list below `md`, with the session's name moved to the meta line beside the kind, because a name such as `job-01m7nwex…` says little. From `md` up the row kept the name as its headline and listed the repositories in the meta line, so the wide list still led with the least readable part of a row.

## Decision

- The session cell of a list row is the same at every width: the headline is `repoHeadline` (owner muted, name bold, "+N" for further repositories), and the meta line is the kind, then the session's name. A session with no repository leads with its name, as before, and its meta line is the kind alone.
- With more than one repository the headline's `title` names them all; the meta line no longer lists repositories.
- The columns from `md` up (state, session, started, running for), the lifetime bar and the five-line arrangement below `md` are unchanged. The session page's own bar is unchanged.

## Rationale

The repository is what tells one row from another at any width, and one rule for the cell is one piece of markup instead of two copies switched by width.

## Consequences

- A session's name is one step less prominent on a wide screen; it is still on the row and is the page's address.
- Titles generated from a session's content would replace the headline's source, not this layout.
