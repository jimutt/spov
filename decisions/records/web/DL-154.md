## Context

A new session opened on a phone showed the server's warning "nothing has been recorded for this session" in the warning colour, and the docked composer sat directly under it near the top of the screen. The dock is `sticky` at the bottom of the transcript column, and a sticky element only moves within its own box: with little in the transcript the box is short and the dock follows the text, not the screen. An empty transcript is the normal state of every session that has just started, so its first screen read as a fault, with the message box in the wrong place.

## Decision

- Below `lg` the transcript column is a flex column at least as tall as the screen under the page's header (`100dvh` less `--header-h` and the top `--main-pad-y`), and the dock takes `margin-top: auto`. It sits at the foot of the screen whatever the transcript holds, and stays `sticky` at the bottom as before once the transcript is long. Everything above the dock sits in one block wrapper, so the transcript's margins collapse as they did. From `lg` up nothing changes.
- The page follows the end on a window `resize` as well as on a body resize, while the tail latch is armed. With `interactive-widget=resizes-content` a phone's keyboard shrinks the viewport, which resizes no element on a long page; without this, the end slips behind the dock as the keyboard opens.
- While the session is live (the composer is drawn for the main conversation) and its transcript is empty, the page drops the server's not-recorded warning (`NotRecorded` in `web/src/lib/timeline.ts`, the same words as `notRecorded` in `internal/server/transcript.go`, guarded by `TestTheNotRecordedWarningMatchesTheBrowser`) and draws a question in its place (`openingAsk`): "What do you want to work on?" in the ink colour, with a soft line under it, centred in the space above the dock on a phone. A job's session, whose goal is already on its way, says the agent is starting on the job instead.
- While the session is `starting` (its sandbox booting, and no composer drawn yet) the same place says "Starting the sandbox", with a spinner (still under reduced motion) and a line saying the message box appears once the agent is ready; a job's says its sandbox is starting. It needs no transcript read, and it is a `status` region, so a screen reader hears the change to the question.
- In both cases every other warning stays, and a session that is neither starting nor live keeps the not-recorded warning as it was.

## Rationale

The warning is true for a session whose recording failed or never began, so it stays for one that is not live; for a live one with nothing yet it describes a beginning. A chat app's empty screen is a prompt to write, and the message box is always at the foot of the screen; the phone layout of DL-106 meant to follow that and missed the short-content case.

`100dvh` follows the browser's toolbars, and under `resizes-content` (Chrome and Firefox on Android) the keyboard too, so the dock rides above it with no script. Safari on iOS does not resize the layout viewport for its keyboard; it scrolls the focused field into view instead, which this leaves as it is.

## Consequences

- The empty state is the browser's decision, keyed on the server's exact words; a rewording of `notRecorded` fails the guard test rather than bringing the warning back silently.
- The column's height now reads `--header-h`, which Base.astro measures; a wrong value shows as a page a little taller or shorter than the screen.
- Checked in a browser at 390 px with a starting, an empty and a long session, and with the viewport shortened as a keyboard would.
